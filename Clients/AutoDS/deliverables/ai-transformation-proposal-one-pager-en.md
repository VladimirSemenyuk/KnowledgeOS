# AutoDS AI Transformation: One-Page Proposal

## Outcome

The program changes the Product and Engineering operating system — not merely its tools — to create more validated product value with the same capacity, shorter time to production, less rework and risk, and disciplined AI investment.

## Three Horizons

| Horizon                            |  Duration |               Fee | Outcome                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | --------: | ----------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Remove constraints**          |  3 months |       **$40,000** | Baseline, ROI model, mandate, decision rights, risk policy, and safe agentic environment. |
| **2. Enable AI-native processes**  |  3 months |       **$40,000** | Repeatable cross-functional workflows, measured impact, and a scale decision.             |
| **3. Institutionalize the system** | 2+ months | **$10,000/month** | Permanent owners, operating cadence, AgentOps cost control, and ROI governance.           |
Baseline duration: **8–9 months**. Minimum program fee: **$100,000**.

Target trajectory: **tens-of-percent delivery gains within six months** and potential **multi-fold growth within 12 months** in mature value streams where the new practices prove scalable.

## Value to AutoDS

- **Business:** earlier outcomes, lower cost of delay, deferred hiring, fewer ineffective pilots, and evidence-based scale/stop decisions.
- **Product:** stronger specifications, faster hypothesis validation, clearer priorities, and delivery tied to customer value.
- **Engineering:** shorter cycles, less rework, earlier quality controls, a Golden Path, safe agent autonomy, and shared standards.

## Why Independent Advisory Is Critical

Agents amplify the system they enter. Without strong specifications, decision rights, quality gates, risk classes, observability, and ROI controls, they can accelerate the wrong work, multiply defects, expose security risks, and create hidden costs.

Tool deployment without management-system change is uncontrolled automation. Vladimir helps leaders redesign that system, challenge cross-functional decisions, establish safe autonomy, verify economic impact, and transfer the capability to internal owners. AutoDS retains program and implementation ownership.
