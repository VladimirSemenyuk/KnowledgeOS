# More Features. Less Time per Feature.

> **Accelerate the path from product intent to a validated feature in production—without increasing defects or rework.**

## 90-day acceleration program

1. **Find the constraint.** Review 3–5 completed features; establish cycle-time, throughput, quality, and rework baselines; select one priority flow.
2. **Accelerate one flow.** Improve agent-ready specifications, controlled agentic execution, shift-left quality, roles, decision gates, metrics, and escalations.
3. **Prove the impact.** Compare results with the baseline and prepare a CTO decision package: scale, adjust, or stop.

**Primary outcome:** lower cycle time and higher validated output. **Guardrails:** defects and rework do not increase. Targets are agreed after the baseline.

## Decision protection

This is a delivery acceleration program, not a test of AI tools. AutoDS continues only interventions supported by its own delivery data. Commercial commitment is staged.
