# Post-Program Governance Retainer

> **Maintain executive decision discipline after the transformation operating model has been transferred to the company.**

An optional continuation of the **AI Delivery Transformation Program** after Embed. It is not a standalone transformation product or an alternative entry point.

## Prerequisites

- A current baseline and transformation roadmap exist.
- Internal owners, decision rights, metrics, and risk controls are operating.
- The company has completed at least two internal management cycles.
- The remaining need is executive oversight—not operating-model redesign or implementation.

## Governance scope

- Regular sessions with the CEO, CIO or CTO, and the internal transformation owner.
- Review of roadmap progress, ROI assumptions, quality, risk, and dependencies.
- Support for decisions to scale, adjust, or stop initiatives.
- Executive Decision Log and Quarterly Transformation Review.
- Selected CEO or board decision materials within the agreed mandate.

## Terms

**Minimum term:** 6 months  
**Fee:** from **$10,000 per month**  
**Scope:** cadence, executive access, decision forums, and support between sessions are agreed at the end of Embed

The retainer does not include daily project management, operating-model redesign, technical implementation, or ownership of execution. If those needs reappear, they are scoped as a new transformation stage.

## Decision at the end of Embed

The client chooses one path:

1. Transfer the governance cadence fully to the internal team.
2. Continue with the post-program governance retainer.
3. Retain individual advisory for one internal owner.
