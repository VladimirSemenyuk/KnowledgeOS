# Executive Technology Advisory

> **A weekly executive counterpart for consequential technology and organizational decisions.**

An individual mandate for one CEO or Technology Executive navigating transformation, reorganization, growth, or a role transition.

## When it is useful

- You need to break through a career ceiling by moving from functional leadership to enterprise influence.
- You face complex business challenges that require an independent executive perspective.
- You need to operate more effectively within the C-suite or with the board.

## What changes

Each week, we work on the live decisions already on your agenda: technology, operating model, organizational change, quality, risk, priorities, and difficult stakeholder conversations.

- You make consequential decisions with clearer trade-offs and less noise.
- You move from reactive control to a stronger executive mandate.
- You connect technology and organizational choices to business outcomes.
- You build the judgment and influence required at C-suite and board level.

## Engagement

- One executive and one 60-minute session per week.
- Preparation and document review within the mandate.
- Unlimited asynchronous access between sessions.
- Additional hours by agreement at **$350 per hour**.

**Minimum term:** 3 months  
**Fee:** from **$1,500 per month**

This is advisory for one executive—not generic coaching, team management, or organizational transformation. An additional executive requires a separate contract. Interviews, workshops, organizational diagnosis, and operating-model change require a corporate mandate.
