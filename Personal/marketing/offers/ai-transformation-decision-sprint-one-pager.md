# AI Transformation Decision Sprint

## Make the investment decision before committing to transformation

A four-week, CEO-sponsored sprint for companies investing in AI without a clear view of what to scale, what to stop, or what must change in their product and engineering operating model.

> **A board-ready baseline, ROI model, and transformation roadmap in four weeks.**

## When this is useful

- AI activity is growing, but business impact remains fragmented.
- Several opportunities compete for investment.
- The CEO, CIO, and CTO do not share one view of the economics, risks, or success criteria.

## The decision you get

The leadership team leaves with evidence to choose one path:

1. **Scale** — fund a 90-day proof stage around the highest-value constraint.
2. **Adjust** — narrow the scope, change the target, or close data gaps first.
3. **Stop** — avoid a transformation without credible economics, mandate, or readiness.

This is not an AI strategy workshop or technical audit. It establishes the investment logic, executive mandate, internal owner, risks, and continuation criteria.

## Four-week process

1. **Align** — define the business pressure, options, and decision criteria with the executive team.
2. **Baseline** — examine 3–5 completed product flows or priority AI initiatives across cycle time, output, quality, rework, cost, and capacity.
3. **Find constraints** — map organizational, process, management, and technical constraints; test ROI scenarios.
4. **Design the path** — define a 90-day proof stage and 6–9 month roadmap with owners, risks, and decision gates.

## Board-ready decision package

- Executive Decision Memo
- Baseline Scorecard
- Constraint Map
- ROI Model with assumptions and required data
- Transformation Roadmap

## Fit and terms

Requires a CEO-level sponsor, access to the CIO or CTO and functional leaders, usable delivery and cost data, and a real investment decision. Technical implementation is not included; delivery improvement is not promised within four weeks.

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Fee:** from **$15,000**; complex portfolios: **$20,000–25,000**  
**Terms:** fixed scope; 100% payment before start
