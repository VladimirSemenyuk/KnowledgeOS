# 2. AI is moving faster than your operating model

| Investment grows | Delivery does not improve | Management load increases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teams adopt AI, but business impact remains fragmented. | Coding gets faster while specifications, testing, architecture, and release remain constrained. | Decisions, quality, and risk still depend on a few senior leaders. |

> The constraint is rarely the AI tool itself. It is usually the system around it: product intent, roles, decision rights, quality gates, metrics, and accountability for the result.

---

# 3. What the transformation changes

```text
AI tools and isolated pilots
              ↓
An explicit baseline and investment thesis
              ↓
An accelerated product-to-production flow
              ↓
Clear roles, decision rights, and quality gates
              ↓
Business metrics and a repeatable management cadence
              ↓
Managed, scalable business impact
```

- **Delivery flow:** less waiting, clarification, rework, and release friction.
- **Operating model:** explicit ownership, decisions, controls, and escalation paths.
- **Management system:** evidence to scale, adjust, or stop AI investments.

# 5. AI Transformation Decision Sprint

## Make the investment decision before committing to transformation

In four weeks, the leadership team gets the evidence to choose one path: **scale, adjust, or stop**.

**Board-ready decision package**

- Executive Decision Memo
- Baseline Scorecard
- Constraint Map
- ROI Model with assumptions and required data
- A 90-day proof design and 6–9 month Transformation Roadmap

**Fit:** CEO-level sponsor, access to the CIO or CTO and functional leaders, usable delivery and cost data, and a real investment decision.

**Terms:** from **$15,000**; complex portfolios **$20,000–25,000**; fixed scope and 100% payment before start.

# 6. AI Delivery Transformation Program

## Prove first. Scale only when the evidence supports it.

| Stage | Focus | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prove — 90 days** | Redesign and test one or two priority flows; establish the baseline and guardrails. | Scale, adjust, or stop. |
| **Scale — 3 months** | Extend proven practices across products and functions; validate ROI. | Confirm the target operating model. |
| **Embed — 2–3 months** | Transfer ownership, decision cadence, risk controls, and the 12-month roadmap. | Exit or continue with governance support. |

**Business result:** more validated product output, shorter time from intent to production, explicit decision rights, protected quality, and an internal management cadence that continues without the advisor.

**Investment:** 90-day stage from **$40,000**; full 6–9-month program from **$90,000**, excluding the Decision Sprint. The client owns implementation; technical delivery and extended program coordination are scoped separately.
