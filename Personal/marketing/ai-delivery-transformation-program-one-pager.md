# AI Delivery Transformation Program

> **Increase validated product output without proportional headcount growth—while protecting quality and manageability.**

A CEO-sponsored program that redesigns the product-to-production operating model, not just the use of AI tools.

## When it is useful

- AI use is growing, but business impact remains fragmented.
- Coding is faster, yet delivery is constrained by specifications, testing, architecture, data, release, or decisions.
- Leadership cannot see which practices to scale, adjust, or stop.

## Program

1. **Prove — 90 days.** Redesign and test one or two priority flows; establish the baseline and decide whether to scale.
2. **Scale — 3 months.** Extend proven practices across products and functions; validate ROI.
3. **Embed — 2–3 months.** Transfer ownership, decision cadence, risk controls, and the 12-month roadmap.

Work spans specifications, agentic execution, quality gates, roles, metrics, and decision rights.

After Embed, external involvement ends or continues through an optional governance retainer. Individual support for one internal owner moves to Executive Technology Advisory.

## Business result

- Shorter time from product intent to production and more validated output.
- Explicit owners, decision rights, quality controls, and escalation paths.
- A verified ROI model and an internal management cadence that continue without the advisor.

Targets are set after the baseline. Defects, rework, incidents, and AI cost remain guardrails.

## Anonymized case

At a US-based company with approximately 60 engineers, selected teams cut the delivery cycle **3×—from three weeks to one** and moved **50–75% of tasks** into an agentic flow.

Vladimir advised engineering leaders on roles, quality, risks, and management cadence. The next constraint shifted to testing and quality governance. The company did not participate in or endorse this publication.

## Fit and terms

Requires a CEO sponsor, an internal owner, baseline data, leader access, and a suitable flow. The client owns implementation.

**90-day stage:** from **$40,000**. **Full 6–9-month program:** from **$90,000**, excluding the Decision Sprint. Post-program governance, implementation, and extended coordination are scoped separately.
