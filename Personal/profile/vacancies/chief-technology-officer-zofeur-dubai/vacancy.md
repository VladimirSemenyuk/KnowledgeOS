## Role Summary

Hands-on, AI-native technology leadership for Zofeur’s next stage of scale.

The CTO will take end-to-end ownership of technology strategy, engineering execution and organizational capability behind a high-volume, real-time mobility platform. The role inherits a live platform and an established distributed engineering team.

The immediate mandate is to:

- strengthen and upgrade the engineering organization;
- internalize critical capabilities while retaining a controlled hybrid delivery model;
- improve delivery speed, quality and reliability;
- reduce key-person dependency;
- embed AI into engineering delivery and mobility intelligence;
- prepare the platform and organization for GCC expansion.

This is not a purely strategic or managerial position. The CTO must be technically credible enough to engage directly with architecture, code quality, infrastructure and production incidents while building the leadership structure required for scale.

## About Zofeur

Zofeur is a mobility technology platform built around delegated mobility for car owners. It provides on-demand chauffeur services, scheduled and subscription-based mobility, enterprise driver logistics and related operational services across multiple markets.

The technology environment includes:

- customer and chauffeur mobile applications;
- real-time dispatch and location systems;
- payments and subscriptions;
- operational control systems;
- enterprise dashboards and APIs;
- analytics and cloud infrastructure.

The company is building its next stage of internal technology leadership as it scales across the GCC.

## What You Will Own

### Technology Strategy and Architecture

- Own the technical vision, architecture and engineering roadmap.
- Ensure the platform supports increasing transaction volumes, geographic expansion and new product lines.
- Review cloud, backend, mobile, data and integration architecture and establish technical standards.
- Prioritize technical debt according to customer, operational and commercial impact.
- Make pragmatic build-versus-buy, modernization and platform-investment decisions.
- Improve systems incrementally, replacing components only where evidence justifies it.

### Engineering Organization

- Assess the existing organization and develop a capability and succession plan.
- Build an internal technology organization while managing a controlled hybrid delivery model.
- Recruit, coach and retain engineering leaders and individual contributors.
- Establish clear ownership across backend, mobile, QA, infrastructure, security, data and AI engineering.
- Eliminate critical individual dependencies and create an accountable and transparent engineering culture.

### Delivery, Reliability and Quality

- Improve engineering velocity without compromising platform stability.
- Establish measurable standards for release frequency, lead time, defect rate, availability and incident recovery.
- Strengthen QA automation across customer, chauffeur, payment, subscription, B2B and operational workflows.
- Implement effective code review, testing, deployment, rollback, observability and incident-response practices.
- Improve technical documentation and engineer onboarding.

### AI-First Engineering and Mobility Intelligence

- Establish AI-native practices across coding, testing, review, documentation, debugging and internal tooling.
- Select and govern AI-development tools with appropriate security, privacy and quality controls.
- Build data engineering, AI engineering and MLOps capabilities.
- Convert high-value AI opportunities into reliable production systems together with Product and Operations.
- Lead initial priorities in automated software engineering and QA, ETA and demand forecasting, pricing and supply planning.

### Cloud, Security and Governance

- Own AWS and GCP architecture, availability, security and cost management.
- Establish identity, access, secrets and production-control policies.
- Ensure company control over source code, infrastructure and critical third-party systems.
- Strengthen cybersecurity, privacy, business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Prepare the technology organization for enterprise diligence, regional expansion and future compliance requirements.

### Executive Leadership

- Act as a strategic partner to the Founder and CEO and challenge priorities constructively.
- Translate company objectives into credible plans, budgets, trade-offs and timelines.
- Represent technology with the board, investors, strategic partners and enterprise customers.
- Build a culture of accountability, urgency, transparency and continuous improvement.

## What Success Looks Like

- Technology ownership has moved from founder-led decision-making to a clear CTO-led operating model.
- Zofeur has a strong internal engineering core supported by governed external capacity.
- Critical systems and responsibilities have documented owners and no material single-person dependencies.
- Release speed, reliability, defect rates and incident recovery are measured and materially improving.
- AI is embedded in everyday engineering delivery and selected mobility-intelligence use cases operate in production.
- The organization supports GCC expansion without proportionally increasing complexity or headcount.

- Decisive, calm and low-ego during incidents and organizational change.
- Pragmatic about architecture and resistant to unnecessary rewrites.
- Comfortable challenging the CEO while remaining accountable for execution.
- Able to explain difficult technical trade-offs in commercial language.
- Motivated by measurable customer and business outcomes.
