# Chief Information & Digital Officer (CIDO)

Источник: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?currentJobId=4441464526)

- **Location:** EMEA-Remote
- **Industry:** Diversified Global Family-Owned Group

## Position Overview

A leading multibillion-dollar family-owned global group is entering a significant transformation phase driven by advances in generative AI, agentic AI, automation, cloud technologies, and enterprise data. The organization is seeking a visionary Chief Information & Digital Officer (CIDO) to lead the next stage of technology-enabled growth and position technology and AI as a strategic engine for business value creation.

This is a transformational leadership role for an AI-native executive who can move beyond traditional IT leadership by embedding artificial intelligence into business strategy, operations, customer experience, and new business models. The successful candidate will lead enterprise-wide digital acceleration, modernize complex technology landscapes, and build scalable AI-enabled capabilities across a diverse portfolio of businesses.

The CIDO will be responsible for creating measurable business impact by transforming technology from a support function into a competitive advantage, while balancing modernization of existing platforms with the rapid deployment of new, cloud-native, AI-driven solutions.

## Key Responsibilities

### AI Strategy & Enterprise Transformation

- Define and execute an enterprise-wide AI and digital transformation strategy aligned with business priorities and growth ambitions.
- Identify, prioritize, and accelerate high-value AI opportunities across business units, functions, and markets.
- Embed generative AI and agentic AI into decision-making, customer engagement, operational efficiency, and innovation initiatives.
- Establish a culture of AI adoption, experimentation, and continuous improvement across the organization.

### Technology Modernization & Digital Enablement

- Develop and execute a comprehensive technology roadmap to modernize legacy systems, reduce technical debt, and improve scalability.
- Transform existing technology environments into AI-ready platforms through cloud adoption, architecture simplification, and infrastructure upgrades.
- Lead the deployment of agile, fit-for-purpose digital solutions for new ventures and emerging markets.
- Create reusable technology models and best practices that can be scaled across the group.

### Data, Analytics & AI Infrastructure

- Build enterprise capabilities across data governance, data platforms, analytics, and AI/ML solutions.
- Establish data as a strategic business asset supporting automation, insights, and intelligent decision-making.
- Develop scalable AI platforms and governance frameworks to enable responsible and secure AI adoption.

### Cybersecurity, Governance & Risk Management

- Ensure a resilient, secure, and scalable technology foundation across infrastructure, architecture, cybersecurity, and information security.
- Establish responsible AI governance frameworks covering ethics, privacy, compliance, and risk management.
- Strengthen technology controls and resilience as the digital ecosystem expands.

### Leadership & Organizational Capability Building

- Build and lead high-performing technology, digital, data, and AI teams across multiple businesses and geographies.
- Develop internal capabilities through talent acquisition, coaching, and upskilling programs.
- Drive collaboration between business leaders, technology teams, external partners, and innovation ecosystems.
- Act as a trusted advisor and transformation catalyst across the organization.

### Value Creation & Strategic Partnerships

- Establish clear measurement frameworks to track ROI and business impact from AI and digital investments.
- Partner with global technology companies, AI innovators, startups, research institutions, and ecosystem partners.
- Ensure technology investments directly contribute to revenue growth, operational efficiency, and competitive advantage.
