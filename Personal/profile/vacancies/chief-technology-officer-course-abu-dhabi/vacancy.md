## Role Purpose

The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) is responsible for defining and executing the organization's enterprise technology strategy, driving digital transformation, modernizing media technology platforms, and enabling innovation through AI, data, cloud, and emerging technologies.

The CTO will lead enterprise Information Technology, Digital Platforms, Data & Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Architecture, and Media Technology functions while ensuring technology investments deliver measurable business outcomes.

The role requires a strategic technology executive with experience leading large-scale digital transformation within complex organizations, preferably digital-first, broadcasting, publishing, media, streaming, or customer-centric environments.

## Key Responsibilities

### Technology Strategy

- Define and execute the enterprise technology strategy aligned with business objectives.
- Lead enterprise-wide digital transformation initiatives.
- Establish technology governance, enterprise architecture, and technology standards.
- Drive innovation using AI, cloud, automation, and emerging technologies.

### Digital & Media Technology

- Lead modernization of digital platforms, websites, mobile applications, CMS, DAM, broadcasting, OTT, streaming, newsroom, and content technologies.
- Improve customer and audience digital experiences.
- Drive adoption of modern content technologies.

### Data & AI

- Define enterprise data and AI strategy.
- Establish governance, quality, analytics, and AI operating models.
- Enable executive decision-making through enterprise analytics.

### Technology Delivery

- Lead complex technology transformation programs.
- Oversee technology portfolios, vendors, budgets, and strategic partnerships.
- Ensure successful delivery of enterprise platforms.

## Required Experience

- 18+ years of technology leadership experience.
- At least 8 years in an executive technology role: CTO, CIO, Chief Digital Officer, Chief Technology & Digital Officer, or equivalent.
- Proven experience leading enterprise digital transformation.
- Experience managing organizations of 100+ technology professionals.
- Experience overseeing multi-million-dollar technology portfolios.
- Strong experience with cloud, AI, enterprise architecture, digital platforms, and data strategy.
- Experience working with executive committees and Boards.
