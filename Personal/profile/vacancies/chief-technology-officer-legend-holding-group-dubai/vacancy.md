## Role Summary

The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) is a strategic executive responsible for driving Legend Holding Group’s technology vision, digital transformation, and enterprise-wide technology enablement.

The CTO will lead the Group’s technology roadmap, platform development, cybersecurity posture, and data-driven innovation to enhance efficiency, scalability, and customer experience across all business units. The role requires a future-oriented, hands-on leader capable of architecting and delivering enterprise systems, digital platforms, and emerging technology initiatives at scale while building a high-performance, security-first technology organization.

## About Legend Holding Group

Legend Holding Group is a diversified enterprise headquartered in Dubai and operating across the Middle East and Africa. Its portfolio includes automotive, trading, energy, travel and tourism, technology, and mobility services.

## Key Responsibilities

### Technology Strategy, Innovation & Digital Leadership

- Define and execute a group-wide technology strategy aligned with business goals.
- Establish a future-ready ecosystem across ERP, CRM, AI, cloud, and applications.
- Advise the Group on emerging technologies.
- Build a strong technology leadership pipeline.

### Enterprise Systems, Applications & Digital Platforms

- Lead ERP, CRM, and enterprise technology rollouts across business units.
- Own development and maintenance of custom business applications, internal tools, and enterprise workflows.
- Oversee consumer-facing applications for customers, dealers, and service operations.
- Develop employee self-service portals for HR, procurement, service requests, and knowledge management.
- Deploy and manage customer- and employee-facing self-service kiosks.
- Integrate ERP, CRM, portals, applications, and kiosks through unified data and identity layers.
- Establish application lifecycle governance, QA standards, release management, and support SLA.

### Infrastructure, Architecture & IT Operations

- Ensure secure, stable, and scalable IT infrastructure.
- Standardize systems architecture across the Group.
- Optimize IT operations and vendor governance.

### Cybersecurity, Data Protection & Compliance

- Own the Group’s cybersecurity strategy, risk appetite, roadmap, and investment priorities.
- Establish an enterprise cyber-risk management framework.
- Implement defense-in-depth architecture, including network security, endpoint protection, IAM, and zero-trust principles.
- Build a 24/7 SOC/SIEM capability and incident-response protocols.
- Own cyber incident response and crisis management.
- Implement and test Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity plans.
- Ensure compliance with UAE data-protection and cybersecurity requirements, including UAE PDPL, ISO 27001, and NIST CSF.
- Establish third-party and vendor risk management.
- Build a group-wide security culture and accountability system.
- Report cyber-risk posture, incidents, and maturity to the CEO, Board, and Audit Committee.

### Data, Analytics & Automation

- Establish data strategy and governance.
- Drive automation, AI, and analytics initiatives.
- Build predictive-insight dashboards.

### Financial Management & Technology ROI

- Manage budgets and technology investments.
- Measure initiative ROI.
- Drive cost-efficient modernization.

### Leadership, Culture & Collaboration

- Improve collaboration across business units.
- Build and mentor technology teams.
- Promote an innovation culture.
